Dream Notes support

A little help along the way.

How do I save my first dream?

Tap Remember a dream, enter what you remember, choose the feeling that stayed with you and pick a cover. Tap Keep this dream. Your saved entries appear in Journal. You can add a personal note under In your own words.

Why is AI dream exploration unavailable?

Optional AI reflection uses Apple's on-device model. It requires iOS 26 or later, a compatible device, Apple Intelligence enabled and the model available. Availability can also depend on system settings, language and region. Open You and tap Refresh availability for the app's current explanation. Writing, saving, searching and editing dreams remain available on iOS 17 or later. Version 1.0 does not connect to a cloud AI service.

How does voice input work?

Tap Speak your dream to start optional English dictation. The app asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission and requires on-device recognition. If permission is denied or on-device recognition is unavailable, continue typing. You can edit the transcript before saving. Dream Notes does not retain the recording.

Where are my dreams stored?

Dreams, drafts, reflections and notes are stored in the app's private storage on your device. There is no Dream Notes account or journal-sync service in version 1.0. iOS device backups may include your journal according to your system settings. Read the privacy policy for details.

How do I edit, share or delete an entry?

Open a saved dream and use its Dream actions menu. Sharing opens the system share sheet, where you choose a destination. To remove the entire journal and unfinished draft, open You and choose Delete all dreams and drafts. Confirming deletion removes those records from the app and cannot be undone there.

Are the examples personal AI readings?

No. The three labeled fictional examples show how the experience works. They are separate from your journal and are not live AI results. Optional generated reflections are possibilities and questions, not predictions, diagnoses or verified facts about you.
