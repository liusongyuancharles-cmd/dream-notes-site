Privacy policy

Effective September 19, 2026 · Dream Notes version 1.0

Dream Notes: Gentle Reflection is a personal dream journal provided by Songyuan Liu. This policy explains the data behavior of version 1.0 and this support website.

Your journal stays in local app storage

Dream text, feelings, personal associations, chosen covers, AI reflections, favorites, drafts and notes are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Version 1.0 has no Dream Notes account, developer-operated journal server or cloud-sync service. The app does not transmit your journal to us.

iOS device backups may include this app data according to your backup settings. Removing the app removes its local journal; restoring a device backup may restore earlier data. Apple manages device backup and restore behavior.

Optional on-device AI

If you choose to explore a dream and a compatible Apple Intelligence model is available, the app uses Apple Foundation Models on your device. Version 1.0 has no configured cloud-reflection endpoint and does not send dreams to a third-party AI provider. The operating system manages model downloads and availability.

AI reflections can be inaccurate. They are optional suggestions for reflection, not verified facts about you or medical advice.

Optional voice input

Microphone and speech-recognition permission are requested only after you start dictation. Dream Notes requires supported on-device English speech recognition. If it is unavailable, you can type instead. Audio is processed for transcription and is not saved as a recording by Dream Notes. The resulting text remains editable and is stored with your draft or dream if you keep it.

Recording stops when you leave the capture page or the app moves to the background. You can manage microphone and speech permissions in iOS Settings.

Sharing and deletion

Nothing is posted publicly by default. Choosing to share a copy opens the iOS share sheet, and the selected destination handles the shared content under its own policies.

You can delete an individual entry through its dream-actions menu, or delete all journal entries and the unfinished draft under You. Deletion removes the selected app records from local storage. Copies you previously shared and existing device backups are managed separately by their destinations or system settings.

Advertising and diagnostics

Dream Notes version 1.0 includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. The app does not write dream content to its diagnostic logs. Apple may provide app diagnostics, crash reports or TestFlight feedback according to your system and testing settings; please review feedback before choosing to share it.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot. Please use fictional examples and avoid sending private dreams. Support correspondence may be retained while needed to resolve your request; you can contact us to request deletion of correspondence under our control, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website

This support website uses no advertising, tracking scripts, analytics tools or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process connection information, such as an IP address, to serve and secure the website. Website hosting does not give us access to your app journal. See GitHub's privacy statement for its handling of website requests.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if a later version changes these practices, including any future cloud-AI or sync feature. This policy describes the current version; it does not claim those future features are enabled.
